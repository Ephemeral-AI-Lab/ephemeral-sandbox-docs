Design thesis

History, bytes, and execution must scale separately

Logical historyMerkle identity stays backend-neutral and metadata-sized.
Physical bytesLocations may change without changing roots.
ExecutionNative projections stay shallow and replaceable.
Eliminate proportional work where reuse is physically possible. Cold reads, cold ingestion, full correctness scans, and portable whole-file copy-up remain honest lower bounds.

# Phase 0 — Independent judge and evidence seal

## Objective

Seal the candidate-independent judge: actual product facts, a finite
mechanically reproducible topology × physical-profile universe, requirements
and scope decisions, fixtures, corpus and untouched holdout, comparators,
thresholds, and provenance. Phase 0 selects no architecture or method and
changes no production V2 path.

The active execution record is the
[`DRAFT / NOT_RUN` Phase 0 packet](../execution/phase-00/00-evidence-seal/packet.md).
Creating or reviewing that packet is not Phase 0 gate acceptance.

## Predecessor outputs consumed

None. Phase 0 creates the inputs every later phase must consume.

## Entry and authorization

- The owner has authorized artifact-system and Phase 0 packet preparation only.
  No durable actor/scope/capability receipt authorizes Phase 0 result-bearing
  work, and no independent phase-entry attestation binds such a receipt to the
  exact actors, scope, capabilities, validity/revocation fact, catalog, gate,
  and lineage. Phase 1A `H_PROFILE` is `NOT_RUN`; the accepted joint,
  architecture, and physical-method winner counts are zero; Phase 1B–1F and
  Phase 2 are unauthorized.
- The documentation worktree, product base, Stage 4.6 evidence identity, and
  any later source-reference commit are recorded separately with dirty-state
  declarations.
- Historical dirty-source receipts are identity commitments, not reproducible
  matched comparator closures unless the exact source bytes and build are
  independently retained and verified.

## Actors and capability ceiling

| Actor | May | Must not |
|---|---|---|
| owner | accept the Phase 0 scope and later accept or reject its sealed output | preselect a candidate, waive a hard rejection, or let this exit authorize Phase 1 |
| independent evidence/judge maintainers | inventory source facts; construct the oracle, fixtures, corpus split, thresholds, candidate closure, and provenance seal | implement or tune a candidate against the holdout; accept their own result |
| candidate authors and implementation agents | supply candidate-identifying material requested under the frozen collection procedure | evaluate, tune, inspect the holdout, add production dependencies, or create authoritative V2 state |
| current operators and writer authorities | provide read-only inventory/evidence under approved procedures | route, select, or acknowledge V2 work |

The Phase 0 capability ceiling is candidate-blind evidence production. A
signature inside Phase 0 can accept only evidence within that ceiling; it
cannot grant candidate evaluation or non-live implementation authority.

## Internal lanes, not top-level phases

Facts/requirements, candidate closure, fixtures/oracle, corpus/holdout, and
measurement preregistration are parallel lanes inside one seal. None may be
changed after candidate results are visible. Any sealed-input change
invalidates every consuming result.

## Work

1. Inventory actual callers, accepted/proposed API names, durable fields and
   roots, writers, leases, replay/effect custody, sessions, checkpoints,
   mutable allocations, runtime effects, storage paths, packages, Cargo edges,
   processes, deployables, and decision/effect authorities from hashed source
   and persisted samples. Keep the e497 18-name manager/runtime catalog,
   eight-name observability registry, HTTP-only `file_list`, HTTP health/forward
   route families, RPC readiness control, legacy rows, proposal rows, and final
   accepted target transport-aware and distinct. Generate direct local normal/
   build/development dependency and source-reference rows from the pinned tree;
   explicitly close every verified provider seed, shared-base builder,
   workspace-capture site, observability DTO/handler, operation consumer,
   dispatcher, route, and control caller. Historical `mpla-poc` and
   `layerstack-core` material creates no current source anchor or edge. The
   generated graph, not a hand-authored example list, is the completeness
   authority.
2. Close the requirements and scope ledger. Resolve `DEC-001`, `DEC-017`, and
   `DEC-018`; name authorization and revocation linearization; and record every
   unsupported behavior rather than inferring it.
3. Freeze the exact MCTS/parallel-rollout contract if required: immutable
   checkpoint parent, sibling/parent isolation, deterministic ancestry and
   result identity, stale-parent outcome, winner/child/sibling fate, no hidden
   merge/rebase, breadth/depth/concurrency/admission/fairness limits, typed
   overload, cleanup, and checkpoint-rooted `commit_fork`. Cover inactive
   fan-out 1/2/4/16/64/maximum plus admitted active rollouts. If MCTS policy is
   out of scope, retain the same bounded generic checkpoint/fork/select/commit/
   prune primitives without putting search policy in storage.
4. Seal the finite control and joint-candidate universe. Record a signed UTC
   submission cutoff; pinned source and submission digests; and generator hashes for
   graph-transform axes, normalized equivalence, admissibility, structural
   dominance, duplicate collapse, and topology × physical-profile expansion.
   First seal compile-valid evidence-only `R_LEGACY_CONTROL` as the unchanged
   current/legacy tree and generate its complete all-`KEEP` dependency/source
   ledger; it supplies comparator endpoints and cannot become `H_PROFILE`. The
   mandatory selectable topology baseline is `R0_IN_PLACE`; every
   `R_ROLE_PARTITION[pi]` covering all five set partitions of baseline ownership
   questions `A`, `S`, and `N`; every finite generated
   `R1_ONE_BOUNDARY[s]`; every `R_PACKAGE_DISPOSITION[d]` and
   `R_PLACEMENT[o,q]` transform over packages, normal/build edges, source sites,
   and consumers present in clean e497; and each `R_NECESSITY_*` whose
   presealed executable trigger fires. Add every complete signed submission
   received before the cutoff and every frozen compatible `T1`–`T8` physical
   profile. Form the physical-profile product only for selectable topologies.
   Require one realization or independently checkable `INAPPLICABLE` proof for
   every generated pair. A late eligible discovery reopens Phase 0
   and requires a new holdout; it is never appended after results are visible.
5. Build byte-exact normal, edge, corrupt, crash, concurrency, migration,
   capacity, request-binding, authorization-race, manager/runtime,
   observability, HTTP route, RPC control, and aggregate-load fixtures with an
   independent oracle. For every accepted no-alias `StateId` binding with
   identical canonical bytes, require checkpoint, reference-only fork,
   rollback-to-existing, `commit_fork` transfer, and same-state reference move
   to have total selected-state
   immutable payload reads = 0, writes = 0, copies = 0, and new root-private
   physical closure bytes = 0. Metadata I/O is measured separately. Seal a
   forced-collision seam that drives unequal canonical bytes under one
   candidate digest through every candidate-bearing admission path—publication,
   import, recovery, index rebuild, and reference-API ingress. Require typed
   `STATE_ID_COLLISION` rejection before any alias, root, mapping, closure,
   revision, custody, or capacity side effect. An accepted reference-only path
   consumes only a previously admitted authorized binding and must not reread
   immutable payload; candidate-bearing reference requests are rejected or
   routed to admission before fast-path entry.
6. Race every reference-only root create/move/ownership transfer against
   removal and retirement of the target closure's last other semantic root.
   Fault validation, retention, bind/move/commit, sync, recovery, root removal,
   reclamation, release, capacity-visible cuts, cancellation, duplicate retry,
   and restart. Require an atomic root-and-retention transfer or durable
   transfer custody before the old last root disappears, released only after
   the new root is durable and capacity-visible.
7. Preregister numeric eligibility and rejection thresholds, statistical
   power, sample size, variance/stopping rules, retry/outlier handling, corpus
   seeds, cache state, warmup/randomization, matched comparator eligibility,
   and disjoint selection and one-time untouched holdout partitions.
8. Freeze toolchain, dependencies, allocator, CPU, kernel, filesystem/mount
   behavior, storage attribution, bytes/inodes/FD accounting, and two distinct
   containment scopes: selected/current storage-side processes/helpers and
   every untrusted runtime's finite parent cgroup or runtime-specific
   containment-equivalent boundary. Record swap, hierarchy/escape,
   descendants, overload,
   supervisor/reaper failure, and cleanup plateaus without implying a new
   service.
9. Freeze authenticated request-key namespaces and immutable normalized
   descriptors, including same-key/different-descriptor, crash, pruning,
   replay, and cross-security-scope cases. Freeze a total classification and
   disposition for every discovered operation, route, and control surface, plus
   deterministic bounded `file_list` behavior if retained.
10. Define disposable Linux, WASI, and Firecracker contract spikes. They may
    falsify a boundary law or return `unsupported`; they neither create
    production runtime-effect implementations nor establish compatibility.
11. Publish every blocker and unknown. Missing facts, an unmatched external
    surface, an
    unavailable matched comparator, or an unbounded resource remains a stop
    condition, never an assumption.

## Deliverables

- content-addressed source/evidence manifests and reproducible commands;
- total caller/external-surface/field/writer/authority/deployment ledgers plus the sealed
  generator for the direct-local-dependency/source-reference ledger and exact
  `KEEP`/`DELETE`/`MOVE`/`NEUTRAL_DTO` dispositions;
- accepted requirements, request-binding, authorization, transport-aware
  surface classification/disposition, bounded `file_list`, and MCTS scope
  contracts;
- finite control, topology, and physical-method universes; control/comparator
  receipts; complete generated selectable joint product; cutoff receipt; and
  equivalence/admissibility/dominance rules;
- fixture corpus and independent expected-result generator; and
- preregistered gate, resource, comparator, statistics, selection-corpus, and
  untouched-holdout contracts with explicit invalidation rules.

## Exit

`QG-PROV-001` passes. `QG-SEM-001` and `QG-PERF-001` record accepted fixture
and threshold contracts, not candidate results. Independent hostile review
confirms the judge does not encode a favored representation and that no winner,
production package, writable V2 state, or future-runtime compatibility claim
was introduced.

## Stop

Stop if actual legacy state, writer/authority/deployment topology, semantics,
MCTS scope, authenticated request binding, authorization revocation,
containment, provenance, comparator, independent oracle, or numeric judge
cannot be established. Do not convert an incomplete baseline into a design
assumption.
